# Crossing

Frogger. Seven lanes of traffic between two banks, a five-way choice every 150 ms, and a three-level
hit-risk score in the same call.

## What the model decides

A choice over `wait`, `forward`, `back`, `left`, `right` (only the moves that stay on the board are
offered), plus the `hit_risk` score. The action is the argmax of the choice.

## The state, as the model sees it

One real tick, seed 7, 6 steps in:

```text
Crossing seven lanes of traffic. You are in lane 1 of 7, 7 rows from the far side. You have crossed 0 times.
```

Verdict first, then the progress, then one number: `deadly: a car is standing on that cell` or `safe:
closer to the far bank, nearest car 4 ticks away`. The long form ("into lane 3, traffic runs right to left,
a car reaches that cell in 2 ticks") made the model pick a cell its own criteria called deadly in 7 of 16
sampled positions; the short form picked none. Arrival times are simulated rather than divided, because
half the lanes only move on every other tick.

## The questions

```json
{
  "move": {
    "type": "choice",
    "instructions": "Pick the move that gets closer to the far bank without being run over. Waiting is only worth it when every step forward is under a car.",
    "criteria": {
      "wait": "safe: no progress, nearest car 3 ticks away",
      "forward": "safe: closer to the far bank, nearest car 5 ticks away",
      "back": "safe: backwards, the start bank has no traffic",
      "left": "safe: sideways, nearest car 2 ticks away",
      "right": "safe: sideways, nearest car 4 ticks away"
    }
  },
  "hit_risk": {
    "type": "score",
    "instructions": "How close is the traffic to the cell you are moving into?",
    "criteria": [
      "the cell is clear",
      "a car is a couple of ticks away",
      "a car is on that cell or arrives now"
    ]
  }
}
```

## The shield

`shield` vetoes a step into a cell a car already stands on or reaches before the next decision, and takes
the highest-probability survivable move instead. With no shield the model is run over within a handful of
ticks; the table shows both.

## The tick

150 ms between decisions. `logic.mjs` is pure and deterministic: the same seed replays the same
episode in node and in the browser, which is what makes the recorded run in `../replay/crossing.json` an exact
rebuild rather than a video.

## What the model actually does

Behind the shield the model crosses 5.6 times per episode against 0.6 for the random policy and 7.25 for
the hand-written one, so the choice question is carrying real information about which cell is safe to step
into — the median is lower (1.5) than the mean because a few runs get far and most end early.
The shield is doing a great deal of that work: 27.5 vetoes per episode, and with the shield switched off
the model is run over after 8 ticks. The `hit_risk` score is not usable at all — it answers the middle
level for almost everything and scores AUROC 0.38 for its top level, which is worse than a coin.
