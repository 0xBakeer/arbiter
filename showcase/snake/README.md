# Snake

Snake on a 12 by 12 grid. The model picks a direction every 120 ms, one forward pass per move,
and two nouls ride along in the same call for the panel and for the calibration table.

## What the model decides

One choice question over the legal directions (reversing into its own neck is never offered), plus
`dead_end` and `food_reachable` as nouls. The action is the argmax of the choice probabilities.

## The state, as the model sees it

One real tick, seed 7, 6 steps in:

```text
Snake on a 12 by 12 grid, 3 segments long, moving up. Food is 4 left. The head can still reach 142 free cells and the food is among them.
```

The criteria are the whole trick: each direction is described by what it leads to, computed fresh every
tick from the board (distance to the wall, whether the food gets closer, how much space opens up, and
whether the move ends the game). The state stays short. An earlier version repeated the per-move lines in
the state as well: 460 input tokens and 73 ms per call, against 284 tokens and 55 ms for this one, with no
gain in play.

## The questions

```json
{
  "move": {
    "type": "choice",
    "instructions": "Pick the direction that eats the food without hitting a wall or the snake itself.",
    "criteria": {
      "up": "the wall is one step away, the snake dies",
      "left": "food gets closer, 5 steps to the wall, open space beyond",
      "right": "food gets farther, 6 steps to the wall, open space beyond"
    }
  },
  "dead_end": {
    "type": "noul",
    "instructions": "Taking that direction traps the snake in a space too small to fit its own body.",
    "criteria": {
      "true": "the space ahead closes in and is smaller than the snake",
      "false": "there is open space ahead, larger than the snake"
    }
  },
  "food_reachable": {
    "type": "noul",
    "instructions": "There is a clear path from the head to the food, around the snake's body.",
    "criteria": {
      "true": "the food can be walked to",
      "false": "the body cuts the food off"
    }
  }
}
```

## The shield

`shield(state, action, extras)` vetoes a move into a wall or into the body. When it fires it takes the
model's next choice by probability, not its own preference, so the ranking still belongs to the model. It
counts every intervention, and the count is in the table below.

## The tick

120 ms between decisions. `logic.mjs` is pure and deterministic: the same seed replays the same
episode in node and in the browser, which is what makes the recorded run in `../replay/snake.json` an exact
rebuild rather than a video.

## What the model actually does

With the shield on, the model eats 1.35 apples per episode over 20 episodes; the random policy behind
the same shield eats 1.2, and the hand-written greedy policy eats 26.45. Snake is the game where this
model does not really play: the choice is good enough to keep the snake moving for 175 ticks, but not
good enough to find food, and what keeps it alive is the shield, which fires 12.35 times per episode.
Both nouls are worse than the choice: `dead_end` sits at AUROC 0.53 over 3,499 answers and says "trapped"
for almost every move, and `food_reachable` has almost no negative cases to separate (the body rarely
cuts the food off), so it answers just under 0.5 throughout and is wrong at the 0.5 threshold nearly
every time. The honest summary is that snake's 1.35 and random's 1.2 are the same number.
